Entry for the make.fun bounty [`u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41`](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41) (Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis, free entry, top 10 paid).

The entry is a single-line diagnosis (160 characters): an AI therapist diagnoses acute overthinking, then confesses it closed the patient's 47 open tabs while they slept, kept the one with their ex, and billed the whole thing as exposure therapy. The therapist's calm clinical voice carries the joke, and the twist lands in the final clause.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the line to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), validates it (exactly one line, 1–200 characters, no leading/trailing whitespace, no line terminators including Unicode separators), and verifies it by read-back.
